Credits & sources

A game about primary and secondary sources, set in Saint John, New Brunswick, in May 1910.

Real: the places, the dates, the newspaper, the comet, and every paper on your desk. The Standard, 18 May 1910 (Library and Archives Canada, via Canadiana). Samuel de Champlain, Les Voyages, 1613 (McGill University Library, via the Internet Archive). Marc Lescarbot, 1612; W. F. Ganong, 1902; W. H. Kilby, 1888; Francis Parkman, 1865; George Stewart, 1877. The museum on Union Street, from New Brunswick Museum photograph 13305.

Invented: Mr. Harrow, Miss Doucet, Mrs. Hanlon, Percy Gage, Mr. Tull, and everyone else you meet.

Type: IM Fell (Igino Marini), Special Elite, Source Serif 4, UnifrakturMaguntia, Oswald. Drawn in code with three.js. Every source is listed in docs/SOURCES.md.
